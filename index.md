# Footclan Bot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11/1/26_

Footclan Bot ("the bot") is a private guild-management bot run by the officers of the Footclan Forever Discord server. It runs in this one server only, is not publicly listed, and cannot be added to other servers. This policy explains what the bot stores, why, and how to have it removed. Our [Terms of Service](terms) cover how the bot may be used.

## What the bot does

- **Characters:** members link their in-game characters with `/link` so officers can see who plays what and invite them to the guild.
- **Guild roster and invites:** matches linked characters to the in-game guild roster, tracks guild invites, and gives guild rank roles.
- **Events and raids:** sign-ups, reminders, bench and attendance.
- **Roles:** role panels where members pick their own roles. Class and profession roles can fill in a member's class and professions.
- **Professions, keys and loot rolls:** a directory of who can craft what, a weekly key board, and `/roll` and `/lootroll` results.
- **Tickets:** private threads for contacting the officers, with a transcript when a ticket closes.
- **Server tools:** scheduled announcements, a moderation log for officers, weekly reset and holiday posts, and shout-outs for in-game achievements.

## What the bot stores

- **Your Discord user ID**, so the bot knows which data is yours.
- **Characters you link:** name, class, spec, role and which one is your main.
- **Event sign-ups and attendance:** which raids and events you signed up for and whether you attended.
- **Your preferences**, for example how you want reminders and whether you want shout-outs.
- **Things you add yourself:** professions, keystones and similar entries.
- **Guild invite tracking:** whether officers invited you to the in-game guild, and short notes officers leave for each other about it.
- **Tickets:** the answers you give when opening a ticket, and a transcript of the ticket thread when it closes.
- **Loot and roll results** from `/roll` and `/lootroll`.
- **Game data** (only when available): level, item level, race, guild and achievements of characters you linked, from Blizzard Entertainment and, where used, Raider.IO and Warcraft Logs.

The bot does **not** store your email address or anything from your Battle.net account. Apart from ticket transcripts, it does not store the content of your messages. For activity statistics it counts messages per channel, without saving what they say.

## Why

The data is used only to run guild features in this one Discord server. It is never sold, rented or shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train machine learning or AI models.

The bot runs on a private server with a private database. Only the bot and the server's admins and officers, through a Discord-login admin panel, can see it.

## How long it is kept

- **Game data** from Blizzard (and Raider.IO / Warcraft Logs) is refreshed or deleted within **30 days**, automatically.
- **Everything else** is kept until you delete it, an officer removes it, or the feature is no longer used.

## Deleting your data

- `/unlink`: remove one character.
- `/deletemydata`: remove everything the bot has stored about you.
- Or ask an officer, who can delete your data for you from the admin panel.

## Game data

Character data from Blizzard Entertainment. Footclan Bot is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard. Blizzard data is provided "as is", without any warranty, and may be incomplete or out of date. Data from Raider.IO and Warcraft Logs belongs to those services and follows the same deletion rules.

## Age

The bot is not meant for anyone under 13. Discord requires all users to be at least 13 years old (or older where local law says so).
